# Turso Design System Analysis

## 1. Core Principles
- **Tinted Dark Hue Consistency**: Every background, card surface, and border is tinted toward deep oceanic teal (`hue ~195°`) rather than untinted `#000000` or `#222222`, creating a cohesive dark atmosphere where the `#4ff8d2` mint accent pops with AAA contrast on dark buttons (`#041014` ink on `#4ff8d2` fill).
- **CLI-First Onboarding**: Product actions are paired 1:1 with their exact CLI equivalent (`turso db replicate`) in a tabbed command strip.
