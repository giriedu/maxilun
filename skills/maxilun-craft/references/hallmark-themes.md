## 2. The 50 Aesthetic Taxonomy Index

1. **Academic Monograph** — Garamond + Source Code Pro, parchment background, margin notes.
2. **Archival Blueprint** — Technical blueprints, blueprint cyan, grid graph backgrounds.
3. **Nordic Minimalist** — Pale birch wood tones, icy sky blue accent, generous whitespace.
4. **Bauhaus Primary** — Pure red, yellow, blue accents, strict geometric circles and rectangles.
5. **Brutalist Newspaper** — Multi-column rule lines, bold mastheads, serif narrative text.
6. **Luxury Boutique** — Bodoni serif display, muted bronze gold, deep velvet black.
7. **Industrial Manufacturing** — Safety yellow, hazard stripes on telemetry, rugged dark steel.
8. **Clinical Bio-Lab** — Crisp clinical white, sterile ice blue, clean sans-serif typography.
9. **Warm Terracotta** — Sunbaked clay surfaces, warm earthy browns, olive green status badges.
10. **Monochrome Noir** — 100% black and white, zero chromatic hue, hierarchy purely by weight and scale.
11. **Botanical Herbarium** — Sage green, forest emerald, botanical illustration slots.
12. **Vintage Terminal CRT** — Amber monochrome CRT phosphor, simulated scanline contrast.
13. **Solarized Technical** — Ethan Schoonover's classic Solarized Dark/Light color physics.
14. **Neon Cyberpunk** — High-contrast midnight black with hot laser pink and electric turquoise.
15. **Mid-Century Modern** — Eames-era mustard yellow, teal, warm teak wood brown tones.
16. **Swiss International** — Akzidenz-Grotesk style, heavy grid discipline, left-aligned sans.
17. **Coffee House / Cafe** — Roasted espresso brown, oat milk foam cream, warm cinnamon accent.
18. **Aviation Cockpit** — Multi-display dark instrument panel, amber alerts, altimeter typography.
19. **Art Deco Geometric** — Gold filigree border accents, geometric symmetry, stylized luxury.
20. **Risograph Print** — Simulated 2-color ink registration, halftone textures, vibrant coral/teal.

## 3. Structural Layout Variety (Ban the 3-Card Cascade)

AI models instinctively generate:
`Centered H1 -> 3-sentence subhead -> Two pill buttons -> 3 identical cards with icons -> CTA`.

**Mandatory Layout Alternatives:**
1. **Split Hero Showcase**: 50% left sticky copy with primary action; 50% right rich live interactive preview.
2. **Asymmetric Bento (2:1 or 3:2)**: Hero spotlight on large anchor cell; peripheral data stacked in side rails.
3. **Tabular Ledger Strip**: Eliminates boxes altogether; uses continuous horizontal dividers with strict columnar alignment.
4. **Editorial Masthead**: Newspaper-style headline spanning the full width, followed by asymmetric 2-column story lead.
5. **Interactive Workbench**: Fixed control sidebar on the left with fluid canvas or data viewport filling remaining space.
